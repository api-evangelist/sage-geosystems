# Sage Geosystems

> Sage Geosystems is a Houston, Texas energy company developing "Pressure Geothermal" — power
> generation and long-duration energy storage built in hot dry rock using subsurface techniques
> carried over from oil and gas. This file is generated by API Evangelist from a public profile of
> the company; it is not published by Sage Geosystems.

## Status

- No public API. Sage Geosystems publishes no developer portal, API reference, OpenAPI/AsyncAPI
  document, GraphQL endpoint, MCP server, A2A agent card, SDK, or `/llms.txt` of its own.
  Probed 2026-08-26; every developer-surface path on www.sagegeosystems.com returned 404.

## Technology

- [Pressure Geothermal](https://www.sagegeosystems.com/technology): two-well power generation
  circulating water through engineered "lung" fractures, with heat extracted at surface and
  converted by an sCO2 turbine.
- EarthStore: single-well geothermal energy storage charging and discharging over 8-hour to
  multi-day timescales.
